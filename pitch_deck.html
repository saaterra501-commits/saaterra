01 • Problem Analysis

The Two-Sided Crisis in B2B Software

Recurring subscription fatigue hurts Indian agencies, while bootstrapped founders starve for distribution.

⚠️ The Agency Struggle (Buyers)
  • Severe Subscription Fatigue: An average boutique agency uses 6 to 8 tools, paying $30–$99/month on recurring foreign credit cards even in slow client months.
  • Zero GST Input Tax Credit (ITC): Foreign SaaS billing gives zero Indian GST tax compliance, causing a direct 18% loss on software expenses.
  • Currency Volatility & Surcharges: Unpredictable USD-INR exchange rate swings and cross-border bank markup fees.
⚠️ The SaaS Founder Struggle (Sellers)
  • High Distribution Barrier: Early-stage founders spend 6+ months building great software but have 0 marketing budget to acquire paying users.
  • Predatory Legacy Platforms: AppSumo and traditional deal sites charge 70% to 80% commissions and hold payouts for 60+ days.
  • Infinite Server Liability: "Lifetime Deals" (LTDs) burden founders with infinite server costs without sustainable boundaries.
